Blinkit's Market Landscape

Quick commerce · Growth team · Cross-category MAC

What quick commerce is now [1][2]

Indian QC has moved from emergency top-up to weekly habit — 10–15 min delivery. Assortment spans electronics, beauty, baby care, pharma.

Blinkit positions as "beyond grocery" — filings cite cosmetics, mobiles, electronics, baby care, bakery. [3][4]

The growth reality [1][5][6][7][8]

  • 53% place 5+ orders/mo; 26% place 10+; 63% weekly [1]
  • 79% grocery · 42% beauty; NIQ: 31% primary grocery · 42% RTE [1][7]
  • Non-grocery ~1.6× faster than grocery (Redseer 2026) [5]
  • ~₹11,000 cr monthly GMV — growth from breadth [5][6]
  • Trust gaps: stockouts, returns, quality, CS [8]
